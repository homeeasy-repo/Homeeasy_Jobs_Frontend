# Blue Lake Revenue Operator Program - Packet I Data Room

This is the public candidate-safe data room for Packet I.

Packet I tests whether a candidate can study an incomplete business context, form a point of view, and explain what they would inspect or build first. It is not the private scoring guide, not an internal knowledge dump, and not a production data export.

## Files

- `01_business_brief.md` - what Blue Lake and HomeEasy are, and where the revenue logic sits.
- `02_assessment_path.md` - the six-stage hiring path.
- `03_packet_i_questions.md` - what candidates must answer in the application.
- `04_ai_and_integrity_policy.md` - allowed AI use and what is tested live without AI.
- `05_packet_ii_build_preview.md` - preview of the later technical build screen.
- `sample-data/applications.json` - fictional candidate/application records for reasoning practice.
- `sample-data/events.json` - fictional event records for reasoning practice.
- `sample-data/school_routes.json` - fictional school/source route records for reasoning practice.

## Not Public

The public room intentionally excludes:

- Internal answer keys and planted gaps.
- Real candidate records.
- Employee files or prior applicant resumes.
- Gmail, WhatsApp, or HR mailbox exports.
- Secrets, credentials, private links, or production database records.

Later packets may use sanitized private material only after a candidate advances and the scope is appropriate.
