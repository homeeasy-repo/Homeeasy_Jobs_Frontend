Commercial founder track. Technical ability is the floor.

Blue Lake is the B2B revenue layer in a US real-estate operating system. HomeEasy is the B2C demand channel. The role sits between software, customer communication, data, and revenue operations.

The title is not a software engineer title because the job is larger than coding. The technical bar is high because the operator must own agentic workflows, debug production behavior, and reason from systems without waiting for a narrow task list.

This is an academy-style entry-level cohort. Blue Lake is not evaluating experienced or lateral hires for this role at this time. The point is to train unusually strong early operators into our way of thinking, building, writing, and owning revenue.

Build and debug systems

Reason about codebases, state, queues, idempotency, data quality, observability, and failure modes.

Move revenue

Understand where the money is made, find leakage, and move the next commercial step yourself.

Communicate externally

Write and speak clearly with renters, buildings, counterparties, and internal operators.

Use AI with judgment

Use AI to accelerate authorized building work. Do not use it to fake understanding.

The technical bar is real.

Everyone who advances can code. The assessment looks for architectural judgment, debugging discipline, and whether you understand what your code is doing when AI helped produce it.

Clear English reasoning matters because the work is US-facing and context-heavy. We test whether you can explain tradeoffs, write plainly, and reason in the business language of the workflow.

Codebase takeover

Read an unfamiliar service, identify state boundaries, patch a bug, and explain the risk.

Algorithmic core

Implement a scheduling or matching primitive with complexity analysis and tests.

Production debugging

Diagnose a failure from logs, schema, and event traces before proposing a fix.

Preview: Signal Engine Challenge

You receive messy lead, building, and commission snippets. Build a small service or notebook that ranks next actions, explains the evidence, and flags missing data.

  • Correct data modeling and edge-case handling.
  • Clear trade-offs, tests, and failure-mode awareness.
  • Commercial logic: why this action makes money or prevents leakage.

No-AI fundamentals gate

Shortlisted candidates complete a live oral screen without AI: data structures, concurrency, databases, debugging, and system design. You must be able to reason under your own power.

  • Explain complexity without memorized theater.
  • Debug from first principles.
  • Defend architecture under changing requirements.
