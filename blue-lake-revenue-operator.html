Not a normal sales role. Not a normal software role.

Blue Lake is the B2B revenue layer in a US real-estate operating system. HomeEasy is the B2C demand channel. The role sits between software, customer communication, data, and revenue operations.

Technical skill is the entry ticket. The job is ownership: finding bottlenecks, building tools, working real counterparties, understanding the unit economics, and moving revenue through the system.

Technical floor

You can code, debug, reason about systems, and use AI tools without surrendering judgment to them.

Commercial ownership

You understand where the money is made and act on the missing step, not only the assigned task.

Communication

You can write clearly and, for phone-facing seats, speak with enough fluency to work US customers and partners.

Remote operating rhythm

You can overlap with US Central Time and carry work without waiting for a narrow task list.

B2B Owner, building, commission, and revenue workflows.
B2C HomeEasy renter demand, qualification, follow-up, and matching.
AI Agents and tools tied to commercial outcomes, not demos.

The process is built to find people who actually think.

AI may be used for building when authorized. It may not be used to fake understanding, summarize material instead of studying it, or manufacture a point of view.
